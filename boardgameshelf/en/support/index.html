What to include

Tell us what you were trying to do, what happened, and what you expected. If relevant, include the app version shown in Settings, your device model and OS version, and steps to reproduce the issue. Include only details you are comfortable sharing.

Screenshots are optional. Hide other people’s names, email addresses and private notes before attaching them. Do not send passwords, payment details or entire rulebooks. Nothing is automatically attached by this page.

Privacy requests

For questions about personal information or requests concerning support emails, use the same address and mention that your inquiry concerns privacy. We cannot remotely view, recover or delete game records stored only on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Sending your message

The email button opens your device’s email app; it does not send a message automatically. If it does not open, copy the address into your preferred email service. This page contains no submission form.

Support is provided by Hikaru Ishiguri (hetobeco). We review messages as we are able; no fixed response time is promised. This is not an emergency contact service.
