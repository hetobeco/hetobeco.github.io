BODOGE SHELF

Privacy policy

This policy explains how Bodoge Shelf (ボドゲ棚) and its support website handle information.

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. Information stored on your device

Information and photos you add are stored on your device and used for app features such as managing games, recording plays, searching and viewing statistics. No account is required, and the app does not provide cloud synchronization with the operator’s servers.

The operator does not receive these records automatically. The app does not include their contents in advertising requests. This does not prevent you from voluntarily including information in a support email.

The app also stores the versions of the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy you accepted and the time of acceptance on your device to remember your acceptance.

2. Camera, photos and device backups

When you add a photo, the app uses the camera or system photo picker. You can manage applicable permissions in your device settings.

Depending on your device settings, information and photos you add may be included in operating-system backups. These backups are managed through the relevant platform’s settings. The app has no independent backup feature and does not guarantee restoration.

3. Advertising

The app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Depending on the SDK, settings and your choices, Google may collect and use IP addresses and approximate location derived from them; device, app or advertising identifiers; usage information such as app launches and ad views or interactions; and diagnostic information such as crashes and performance data. This information is used for advertising, measurement, analytics, service improvement and fraud prevention. Information may also be shared with Google's advertising partners to deliver and measure ads. The app does not request precise GPS location.

The app uses Google's consent management service (UMP) to manage advertising privacy choices. Depending on your region and applicable settings, a privacy message may be displayed. Where the app's settings show Privacy options, you can use it to review or change your choices.

See Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps. Non-personalized advertising can still involve data processing.

4. Website and support inquiries

The support and legal-information pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, including when opened inside the app. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement. These pages contain no additional analytics, advertising tags, third-party fonts or embedded contact form.

If you email hetobeco@gmail.com, the operator receives your email address, sender name, message and any attachments you choose to send. Gmail is used to receive and manage these messages. Information is used to respond, investigate problems and handle privacy requests. App records and photographs are not attached automatically. You can use the app without contacting support.

5. Retention and deletion

You can edit and delete information and photos through the app. To remove all local app data, use your operating system's option to delete the app together with its data; offloading the app may keep its data. Deleting data or the app does not automatically remove original photos from your photo library or operating-system backups.

We retain inquiry information for as long as needed to respond and resolve related issues, and delete it when it is no longer needed. Information required to meet legal obligations or address disputes is retained for as long as necessary for those purposes.

You may request deletion of support information by email. The operator cannot remotely inspect or delete records stored only on your device. Data retained by Google, GitHub or your backup provider is governed by the relevant service’s policy and available controls; we do not promise that uninstalling the app deletes it.

6. Sharing, security and processing locations

The operator does not sell saved game records or support emails. Support information is shared with Google to receive and manage email, and otherwise only where necessary to comply with law or protect legal rights. See sections 3 and 4 for advertising and website data processing.

Local data uses your device’s app-storage protections. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Google and GitHub may process information in the United States and other countries outside your country of residence. See their privacy policies linked above for details.

7. Your requests and younger users

You can contact hetobeco@gmail.com to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held by the operator, or to ask about other rights under applicable law. Requests are handled subject to applicable conditions and identity checks limited to what is necessary. Information required to answer an inquiry is voluntary, but withholding it may prevent us from investigating the issue.

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed primarily to children. If you believe a child’s personal information has been sent to the operator, please contact us. We will review the matter and take appropriate action, including deletion where required by applicable law.

8. Changes and contact

When this policy changes, the revised version and effective date will be posted on this page. Additional notice or consent will be provided where required by applicable law.

Operator: Hikaru Ishiguri (hetobeco). Contact: hetobeco@gmail.com.
